# Known limitations

Status: these limitations are **known**, not solved by marketing claims.

- Overhead CCTV may not show small phones clearly.
- Pose can be unavailable due to occlusion.
- Anonymous sessions may split one real worker into several sessions; sessions are not automatically merged.
- Face snapshots are visual references, not identity recognition.
- Camera coverage does not equal complete knowledge of work.
- Customer-service events require good zones and tracking.
- Unknown time must not be interpreted as idle.
- Real-pharmacy accuracy, fairness, latency and failure behavior must be measured before production claims.
- The included tracking and inference adapters require real-site validation and are not a safety-certified system.
